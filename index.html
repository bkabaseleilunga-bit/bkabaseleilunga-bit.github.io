About

From the hospital ward to the emergency operations centre

I am a medical doctor based in Kinshasa. I began as a hospital physician in Mungbere, Haut-Uélé, then moved into outbreak response: Ebola in Butembo and Mbandaka, COVID-19 with UN Volunteers, then Mpox, measles-rubella and cholera.

Since October 2024 I have worked at the COUSP, where I turn surveillance data into products that leaders use: SPOTREPs, the weekly surveillance bulletin, risk assessments and dashboards. I build these in R and Quarto so they can be regenerated automatically, and I train and mentor the people who will run them. Since October 2026, I am also a CGPP consultant (CORE Group Partners Project) in Tshopo, coordinating implementing partners to prepare health zones not affected by the Ebola outbreak.

I am completing an MPH in Biostatistics at the Université des Montagnes. My research builds an integrated tool to predict the risk of zoonotic outbreaks, combining surveillance, climate, environmental and health-zone vulnerability data with socio-anthropological factors.

Capacity building and accompaniment

Training design and delivery, hands-on mentoring of surveillance and data teams, and SOPs that make handover last.

Collaborative surveillance and coordination

Indicator-, event- and community-based surveillance, IDSR, Incident Management Systems and coordination with WHO, Africa CDC, US CDC, UNICEF and other partners.
